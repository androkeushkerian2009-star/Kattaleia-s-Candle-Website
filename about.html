About Us

Welcome to Kattaleia's Candle & Sculptures! 🕯️🎨 Our candles are made with love, care, and the finest ingredients to bring warmth and joy to your home. Every scent tells a story — whether it’s relaxing lavender, sweet vanilla, or fresh citrus, our candles are designed to make every moment special. In addition to candles, we also create beautiful hand-crafted sculptures that add a unique artistic touch to your home. Each piece is made with passion and attention to detail, reflecting creativity and elegance. Started from a small passion at home, we now create candles and sculptures for friends, family, and customers who want to light up and decorate their spaces with comfort and style. Thank you for visiting and supporting our artistic journey. We hope our creations bring as much happiness to you as they do to us! ✨
